# RamLume 0.1.2

A small update to RamLume 0.1.1: the dashboard does less work while it's open, and several
small bugs are fixed.

## What's new

- **A lighter dashboard.** Pages that update every second were measuring their layout several
  times per update. Now they measure it once. On Advanced › Memory in a large window that's
  roughly half the work.
- **A lighter panel in some cases.** With only the menu bar panel open, RamLume no longer keeps
  a Window menu registered with macOS, which could cost extra work each time the panel updated.

## Fixes

- Charts now update at once after you clear history, pause or resume monitoring, or change how
  long history is kept. They used to wait for their next timed redraw, up to a minute.
- An app that quit while RamLume was scanning is no longer counted as "restricted".
- Notifications no longer go silent after the Mac's clock is set back.
- Wording: "across 1 processes" is gone, and VoiceOver no longer reads "1 processes".
- Sizes just under a unit boundary read "0.98 GB" instead of "1,000 MB".
- With another user's session in front, RamLume stays on its slow idle sampling when the
  displays wake.

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

## Updating from an earlier version

Quit RamLume, replace the copy in your **Applications** folder with the new one, and open it.
Your history and settings carry over.

## Before you open it

<!-- Keep this section for an ad-hoc signed build. For a build that make-release.sh reported as
"Developer ID signed and notarized", replace it with:

This build is **not notarized** by Apple, so macOS stops the first launch of each new version:

1. Unzip the download and move **RamLume** to your **Applications** folder.
2. Open RamLume and dismiss the warning.
3. **macOS 15 or later:** open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about
   RamLume, click **Open Anyway**, and confirm.
   **macOS 14:** Control-click RamLume in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/13nik09/ramlume/issues/new/choose) and include
the output of `/Applications/RamLume.app/Contents/MacOS/RamLume --dump-metrics`.
